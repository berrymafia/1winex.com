What Aviator is

Aviator is Spribe's crash game. You play it at 1win with the same account as slots and sports, but it isn't a 1win Original. Lucky Jet is the crash title in that collection — same kind of round, different studio and rules.

Everyone at the table plays the same round. The plane takes off, a multiplier climbs, and you cash out while it's still going. Wait too long and that stake is gone. Rounds are random, so a run of high numbers doesn't tell you what comes next. RTP, stakes and the full rules sit in the game info panel.

How Aviator works

Every round is separate. Cash out while the plane is flying, or you lose that stake.

1

Set a stake

Pick a stake in the game. Limits are in the panel. You can usually put one or two bets on the same round.

2

The plane takes off

The plane takes off and the multiplier starts at 1×. It climbs while the plane is flying.

3

Cash out

Tap cash out to lock the current multiplier. Wait longer for a higher one — or lose the stake if the plane flies away first.

4

The round ends

Any stake you didn't cash out is lost. The next round starts on its own timer.

Cash out at 10× and that stake is paid ten times. Miss it and that stake is gone. The next round does not owe you a high multiplier.
