How a Lucky Jet round works

Each round is independent and starts at 1×.

You can usually place one or two bets in the same round. Set and cash out each one separately.

1

Choose your stake

Enter the amount before the round starts. If two bet panels are available, each bet can use a different stake and cash-out setting.

2

Watch the multiplier

Lucky Joe takes off and the multiplier rises from 1×. The round can end at any moment.

3

Cash out in time

Press Cash out to lock the displayed multiplier for that bet. Waiting may increase the payout, but you lose the stake if the round ends first.

4

Review the result

Cashed-out bets settle at their locked multiplier. Any bet still open when Lucky Joe flies away is lost; the next round is independent.

Example: cashing out at 10× returns ten times that stake in total, subject to the game rules. It does not affect the next round.
