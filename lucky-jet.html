What Lucky Jet is

Lucky Jet is the crash game in 1win Originals — same account as slots and sports. Aviator looks similar, but that's Spribe, not a 1win Original.

Everyone plays the same round: the jet takes off, the multiplier climbs, you cash out while it's still going. Wait too long and that stake is gone. A streak of high numbers doesn't tell you the next one. RTP, stakes and the rules are in the game info panel.

How Lucky Jet works

Each round stands on its own. Cash out while the jet is flying, or that stake is gone.

1

Set a stake

Choose a stake in the game. Limits are in the panel. You can usually put one or two bets on the same round.

2

The jet takes off

The multiplier starts at 1× and climbs while the jet is in the air.

3

Cash out

Tap cash out to lock the current multiplier. Waiting can pay more — or you lose the stake if the jet flies away first.

4

The round ends

Anything you didn't cash out is lost. The next round starts on a timer.

Cash out at 10× and that stake pays ten times. Miss it and it's gone. The next round doesn't owe you a high multiplier.
