KYC & Identity Verification

Know Your Customer (KYC) is requested at the first withdrawal, not at signup. That is the same path we walked on the cashier: photo ID plus proof of address. Payouts stay held until documents land.

In our test, KYC cleared in 26 hours. Operators often quote 24–48 hours. Three-rail payout table: withdrawal times.

Delayed KYC lets you look around before documents. Submit them before a large cash-out if you want to avoid a freeze at withdrawal.

Security Measures

We opened the site over HTTPS. The certificate we saw used TLS 1.3. No mixed-content warnings on the registration, login and cashier pages we used. That is a transport check, not a penetration test.

Account-level security includes optional two-factor authentication (2FA). Turn it on. The platform can flag odd logins (new device, unusual location). We did not test every freeze rule.

We did not find 1win in public breach dumps at review time.

Fair Play & RNG

Fairness at 1win is split. Third-party slots and live games use the studios’ own RNGs (Pragmatic Play, Evolution, NetEnt and others). Those labs sit with the providers, not as a 1win site-wide certificate.

1win Originals (Lucky Jet and similar in-house titles) are provably fair. Aviator is Spribe, not an Original — same split as the game catalog. We checked that Originals expose a seed-verify flow; we did not run a laboratory RNG audit.

Dispute Resolution & Support
