KYC & Identity Verification

Know Your Customer (KYC) is requested at the first withdrawal, not at signup: photo ID plus proof of address. Payouts stay held until documents land.

KYC typically clears in 24–48 hours. Three-rail payout table: withdrawal times.

Delayed KYC lets you look around before documents. Submit them before a large cash-out if you want to avoid a freeze at withdrawal.

Security Measures

The site runs over HTTPS with TLS 1.3 on registration, login and cashier. Mixed-content warnings are not shown on those pages.

Account-level security includes optional two-factor authentication (2FA). Turn it on. The platform can flag odd logins (new device, unusual location).

Keep 2FA on and finish KYC before a large cash-out.

Fair Play & RNG

Fairness at 1win is split. Third-party slots and live games use the studios’ own RNGs (Pragmatic Play, Evolution, NetEnt and others). Those labs sit with the providers, not as a 1win site-wide certificate.

1win Originals (Lucky Jet and similar in-house titles) are provably fair. Aviator is Spribe, not an Original — same split as the game catalog. Originals expose a seed-verify flow.
