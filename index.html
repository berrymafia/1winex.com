License Verification

1win operates under a Curacao eGaming license (number 8048/JAZ), issued by Antillephone N.V. This is one of the most common licenses in the online gambling industry. It is a legitimate, verifiable license, but it is important to understand what it does and does not guarantee:

What the Curacao License Covers

The Curacao license is real and verifiable, but it is weaker than licenses from the UK Gambling Commission or Malta Gaming Authority. Players in jurisdictions with stronger local regulators (UK, Sweden, Germany) should be aware that 1win is not licensed by those bodies, and disputes are handled through the Curacao channel. How the Wagering Actually Works

Unlike most casino bonuses, 1win's welcome offer clears through sports betting rather than slot play. Wagering is x5 on sports bets (minimum odds 1.3, typically 3+ event accumulators) with a 30-day window. Losing bets do not contribute toward clearing.

This structure favors sports bettors over slot players. If you don't bet on sports, the welcome bonus clears very slowly through casino play and may not be worth claiming. Read the terms carefully before opting in.
